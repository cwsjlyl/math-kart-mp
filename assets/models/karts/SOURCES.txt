Classroom kart models (compact .glb)

  Derived from Kenney Car Kit 3.1 (www.kenney.nl), Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
  Recoloured / retargeted for this classroom game. 赤電號 window-eyes and 07
  markings are original additions, not official commercial characters.

- bike (冒險單車):
  Derived from the Pixor library bicycle
  (https://pixor.art/library/vehicles/bicycle/), Creative Commons CC0 1.0,

No official commercial racing IP was used.
